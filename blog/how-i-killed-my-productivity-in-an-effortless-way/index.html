How I killed my productivity in an effortless way

23 Apr 2024 · productivityacademia

Originally published on Medium on 23 April 2024.

I confess that for a few weeks/ months I had forgotten that my current job as a researcher in this field is the dream job I’ve been fighting for years to get. As you may guess, it affected my performance tragically! A few months ago, I looked super energetic behind my desk. Sometimes, music was being played in my earphones, and I was slightly rocking back and forth in my chair like I was dancing. Yes, I was really enjoying myself doing what I’ve been doing. It went this way for almost the first 19 weeks of being in my current position. But after a while, I realised that I was becoming a worried, anxious, and negative person who may be nagging about his way of working from one time to another or blaming his surroundings for his unproductive days and his procrastination.

During my holiday, I decided to dig deeper into my recent performance to find out what had been wrong with me. I discovered a few behavioural patterns that had been replaced and I still cannot believe what a huge difference they made.

1. Stay away from negative-vibed social discussions as much as possible!

In social circumstances, when a negative conversation was about to start, in most cases, I used to close my ears gates and pretend to listen rather than really listen. As soon as I started being an active listener or contributor in these conversations, my focus and productivity started to drop. Well, contributing to negative conversations doesn’t improve my credit score. So, I just decided to return to my pretender self in these circumstances! :D

2. Focus on the beauties of the road; stop thinking about the magnificence of the destination!

For quite a time, I used to cycle 100 km per day on average for five days a week. One thing that I learnt quite early was that as long as I count down the remaining kilometres, I’m gonna drop. If I focus on enjoying the beauties around me on the road, not only will I be happier and more joyful during the ride, but I’ll also finish the ride with less mental suffering.

When I imagine the beauty of my destination, it might be motivating at a glance, but thinking too much about it will distract me from the current beautiful view I’ve got and at some points, I may struggle; I may feel like asking myself: “Are you sure this destination is real? Are you sure it’s worth this pain, sweat, and heart rate?” And I’ll probably be done doing it and will make a U-turn to home! If a final destination or an intermediate destination is gonna be scary and I think about it too much, well… You guess the rest!!!

Well… I realised that recently, I’ve been talking a lot about my annual review milestone, and I couldn’t work effectively to prepare for it! What a ridiculous joke!!!

Further, I have been so focused on counting the words and pages I’ve been writing that I totally forgot that I’m supposed to produce quality documents!

So, I decided to get back to my former attitude. I know I should prepare for the annual review, but I don’t worry about it. It will be whenever it is meant to be! :) If I enjoy doing what I’m doing daily and am honest with myself about my performance, then there’s nothing to worry about for any review meeting! :)

3. Break boring tasks down! Break them more! Even more! Even more! Yep! That’s it!

Regardless of what we do for life or how much we love it, there are always some boring tasks that we should get done! For me, they are the least motivating ones to deal with. That’s when I procrastinate more than my usual procrastinating times.

Taking care of these tasks could be easiest for me when I break them into smaller tasks? How much smaller? I’ve discovered that I can tolerate doing what I don’t like doing for a maximum time of 10 minutes non-stop! So, if these micro-tasks are gonna take more than 10 minutes on average, it means that I should break them down into new tasks even more!

This way, I face a long list of DONE tasks at the end of the day, many of which are not my favourites. This boosts my motivation and confidence daily! I don’t know how effective it is gonna be for you, but you may wish to give it a shot for one or two days! :)

4. My phone still has the “Do not disturb” button!

My phone used to turn “Do not Disturb” mode automatically on, whenever I arrived at the university campus. A while ago, while waiting for a phone call, I turned it off, and guess what? I didn’t turn it on voluntarily for a long time, and I easily got distracted by every single notification, even when in silent mode! I just got the automation back this Monday! It helps a lot! :))

5. Playlist matters!

Yes! Music matters a lot! Be mindful about your playlist and music selection! I’m not gonna explain how listening to the wrong music at work killed my productivity and my time for sure!

You may wish to listen to the TEDX talk By Dr. Lee Bartel at the link below:

Dr. Lee Bartel: Music Medicine: Sound At A Cellular Level

Question:

Am I the best or most productive version of myself now?!

Answer:

Absolutely not! There is still a long way to go. The best version of myself is the version I’ve not met yet! But, for now, at least I’m not the worst version of myself and am back on track to getting to my best version! :D

What creates versions of us? Our small habits, for sure! And I just mentioned a few little habits that made a huge difference in my performance. I’m not sure if they’re gonna be efficient for you as well or not, but as I said, you may wish to give some a shot.

What I’m sure is that anyone has their own way of performing productively; they just may not have found it or may get lost at some point. It’s important to share our struggles and how we dealt with them as much as we share our achievements. Especially when it comes to academia, in which the nature of the work is to deal (or to struggle at some points :D) with complex or wicked problems. There is no journal to share our failed research attempts! So, this may make some of us quite perfectionists.

Everyone has good days and bad days! It does matter for sure! But, what matters more is to learn how we could turn more bad days into good days.

Take it easy, and find your own way of having fun, enjoying yourself, and getting your work done!
